Wind farm cluster available datasets

List of open source wind farm datasets：

1.The dataset on wind farm clusters in China’s eastern coastal region originates from a competition project within Digital China Innovation Contest
(https://www.dcic-china.com/competitions/10098).

-includes 5 Wind farms，actual electricity generation and 10 meteorological variables
the period from 3 January 2022 to 30 April 2023 with a 15-minute resolution

2.The dataset on wind farm clusters in the Pakistan region originates from a corporate project on the Kaggle website     (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mubashirrahim/wind-power-generation-data-forecasting).
 
-includes 4 Wind farms，actual electricity generation and 8 meteorological variables 
the period from  2 January 2017 to 31 December 2021 with a 1-hour resolution,
The power generation data were pre-normalized
